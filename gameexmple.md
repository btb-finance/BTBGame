# BEAR & Hunter Ecosystem: Usage Examples

## Example 1: Depositing a BEAR NFT

**Scenario: Alice's First Deposit**

Alice owns 3 BEAR NFTs in her wallet. She decides to deposit one to start playing the game.

1. Alice connects her wallet to the Cave interface
2. She selects BEAR #4217 to deposit
3. She approves the transaction
4. The Cave sends BEAR #4217 to the BTBSwap contract
5. Alice immediately receives:
   - 1,000,000 MiMo tokens in her wallet
   - Hunter NFT #8391 with base hunting power (10 MiMo/day)
6. Alice can now begin hunting and feeding her Hunter

## Example 2: Hunter Progression Over Time

**Scenario: Bob's Hunter Growth Journey**

Bob has had his Hunter NFT for several months and is diligent about daily feeding.

1. **Day 1:**
   - Bob's Hunter hunts 10 MiMo tokens
   - Bob receives 5 MiMo (50%)
   - 2.5 MiMo are burned (25%)
   - 2.5 MiMo go to liquidity pools (25%)
   - Bob feeds his Hunter, increasing power by 2%

2. **Day 30:**
   - Hunter power has grown to hunt 17.39 MiMo per day
   - Bob receives 8.7 MiMo (50%)
   - 4.35 MiMo are burned
   - 4.35 MiMo go to liquidity pools
   - Total accumulated: ~438 MiMo

3. **Day 180:**
   - Hunter power now at 277.10 MiMo per day
   - Bob receives 138.55 MiMo daily (50%)
   - Total accumulated: ~12,127 MiMo
   - Bob's Hunter has become significantly more valuable

4. **Day 365:**
   - Hunter power peaks at 9,003 MiMo per day
   - Bob receives 4,501.5 MiMo daily (50%)
   - Total lifetime earnings: ~1,000,000 MiMo
   - Hunter reaches end of lifespan

## Example 3: The Effects of Missed Feedings

**Scenario: Charlie's Inconsistent Feeding**

Charlie has a Hunter NFT but isn't always consistent with feeding it.

1. **Normal Progression:**
   - Day 45: Hunter hunts 22.56 MiMo
   - Charlie feeds the Hunter afterward
   - Hunter power increases to 23.01 MiMo

2. **First Missed Feeding:**
   - Day 46: Hunter hunts 23.01 MiMo
   - Charlie forgets to feed the Hunter
   - Day 47: Hunter power remains at 23.01 MiMo (no growth)
   - Charlie feeds the Hunter again
   - Power increases to 23.47 MiMo for next day

3. **Multiple Missed Feedings:**
   - Days 60-66: Charlie goes on vacation, doesn't feed Hunter for 7 days
   - Hunter enters hibernation after 7 missed feedings
   - Day 67: Charlie returns, feeds Hunter
   - Hunter exits hibernation but needs 24 hours to recover
   - Day 68: Hunting resumes but power has decreased by 30%
   - Several weeks of consistent feeding needed to return to optimal growth

## Example 4: MiMo Token Redemption

**Scenario: Diana's BEAR NFT Redemption**

Diana has accumulated 1.2 million MiMo tokens and wants to redeem them for a BEAR NFT.

1. Diana connects to the Cave interface
2. She initiates BEAR NFT redemption
3. The system requires 1.1 million MiMo tokens:
   - 1 million MiMo tokens are burned
   - 100,000 MiMo tokens (10%) are sent to liquidity pools
4. Diana receives a BEAR NFT from the BTBSwap contract
5. Diana now has:
   - 1 BEAR NFT (worth approximately 1M MiMo in value)
   - 100,000 MiMo tokens remaining in her wallet

## Example 5: Multi-Hunter Strategy

**Scenario: Evan's Hunter Portfolio**

Evan has deposited 5 BEAR NFTs over time and manages multiple Hunters at different stages.

1. **Hunter #1: Day 300**
   - Hunting capacity: 2,538 MiMo per day
   - Near end of lifecycle
   - Generating substantial daily rewards

2. **Hunter #2: Day 180**
   - Hunting capacity: 277 MiMo per day
   - Midway through lifecycle
   - Showing accelerating returns

3. **Hunters #3-5: Days 1-30**
   - Recently created
   - Low initial returns (10-17 MiMo per day)
   - Being positioned for future growth

4. **Daily Routine:**
   - Evan feeds all 5 Hunters
   - Total daily hunt: ~2,860 MiMo
   - Evan's strategy ensures continuous high rewards even as older Hunters expire

These examples illustrate how players would interact with the game ecosystem and the strategies they might employ to maximize their returns while engaging with the core mechanics.
